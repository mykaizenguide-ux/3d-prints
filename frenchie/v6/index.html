Low Poly Frenchie

Rendition v6 (2026-10-01): reconstructed from one reference picture, most facets on the head. The hand-placed planes of v1 to v5 could not make the pushed-in face, the brow wrinkle or the scooped ears, so this one is reconstructed from the reference picture and reduced to about 3,500 flat facets, 1,800 of them on the head where the eyes, brow and jowls are. The belly is opened up underneath, and low edges are filled straight down to the bed.
