Spectacle Tree

A bare low-poly tree with two nose-saddle boughs, a low one forward and a high one back, that parks two pairs of glasses. Each saddle is a 17 mm ridge the nose pads straddle, with a V notch that seats the bridge bar so the frame cannot yaw or slide; lenses hang in free air. Drag to spin, scroll to zoom; the Glasses button shows a pair on each bough.
